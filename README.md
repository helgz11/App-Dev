
# Even If This Love Disappears Tonight

## **About the Movie**

*Even If This Love Disappears Tonight* is a **2022 Japanese romantic drama film** directed by Takahiro Miki. It tells the story of two high school students, Toru Kamiya and Maori Hino, who develop a special relationship despite the challenges they face.

### Movie Description

Maori Hino has a condition called anterograde amnesia, which causes her to forget the events of the previous day when she wakes up. Toru Kamiya gradually falls in love with her and tries to make every moment they spend together meaningful. Their story explores the importance of love, memories, and appreciating the people who matter most to us.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because it shows that love is not just about being together but also about making someone happy and creating meaningful memories. It reminds us to appreciate every moment with the people we love.

- **Maori Hino** - A cheerful girl who experiences memory loss but continues to find happiness.
